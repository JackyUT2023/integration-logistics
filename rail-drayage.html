Rail Drayage

CN and CPKC rail drayage services from major rail terminals to consignee destinations across Canada.

Container pickup and delivery

We coordinate container pickup, pre-pull, yard storage arrangements, appointment deliveries and communication between truckers and consignees.

  • CN rail terminal pickup
  • CPKC intermodal pickup
  • Container delivery scheduling
  • Yard storage and pre-pull coordination
  • Status follow-up and communication

Typical markets

Toronto, Montreal, Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Winnipeg and surrounding commercial markets.

For rail shipments, useful details include container number, rail terminal, delivery address, cargo weight and receiving appointment requirements.

Terminal availability

Drayage dispatch

We support ETA updates, receiving coordination and delivery confirmation.
