Resources

Helpful logistics references for importers, exporters and customers arranging shipments into Canada.

What is Rail Drayage?

Rail drayage refers to transportation between rail terminals and the final destination, warehouse or delivery point.

What is a PARS Number?

PARS allows customs information to be reviewed before release and pickup timing is arranged where applicable.

What is a SOC Container?

SOC means Shipper Owned Container. Handling and return arrangements may differ from carrier-owned equipment.

What should I include in a quote request?

Origin, destination, commodity, weight, dimensions, shipment type and special delivery requirements are all helpful.
