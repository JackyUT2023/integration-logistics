Quick logistics references for importers, exporters and customers arranging shipments into Canada.

What is Rail Drayage?

Rail drayage refers to the transportation of containers between rail terminals and their final destination, warehouse, yard or distribution point.

What is a PARS Number?

PARS allows customs documentation to be reviewed before cargo arrives at the Canadian border or before pickup is arranged where applicable.

What is a SOC Container?

SOC stands for Shipper Owned Container. These containers may have different handling and return requirements compared with carrier-owned containers.

What information is needed for a freight quote?

Helpful details include origin, destination, commodity, weight, dimensions, shipment type, reference numbers and any special handling requirements.
